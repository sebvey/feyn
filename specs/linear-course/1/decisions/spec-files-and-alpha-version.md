Without this, an agent would add a rule as one more sentence of `SPEC.md` prose, beside the
text that explains it, where no check can select it by category or count it, and would keep
numbering the spec `1.0.0` while its content changes from commit to commit, or move it to
`0.y.z` and rename `1/` and `@1` twice (working notes D6, D13).

The files of `linear-course` are split by class of products (decision
`rules-with-level-and-method`), plus the frame. `SPEC.md` holds the frame: scope, conformance
clause, levels, methods, categories, how severity follows, versions, the normative references,
the list of checks; it holds no rule, or only rules on the spec itself. `COURSE.md` describes
what a course is (today's `SPEC.md` sections 2 to 7 and 9, the language pack and the diagrams
included); its rules are in `rules/course.yaml`, in English, and its tables of fields
(`course.yaml`, the `concept.md` frontmatter, the keys of the pack) are schemas in
`schemas/*.schema.yaml`, JSON Schema 2020-12 written in YAML, each schema counting as one rule
("`course.yaml` is valid against its schema"); the rule file keeps what a schema cannot say
(the order of the glossary, the contract of a case, the sources). `PROTOCOL.md` describes the
protocol of the teacher (Purpose, notation, keys and parameters); its rules are in
`rules/protocol.yaml`, in French, the bounded exception of decision `language-policy`. A
description (`SPEC.md`, `COURSE.md`, `PROTOCOL.md`) is informative: it explains and cites rule
ids, and never uses the verbal forms of obligation; the rule files are normative. "teacher"
stays the name of the class of products, "protocol" the name of its file and description.

The version: a MAJOR in preparation carries `<MAJOR>.0.0-alpha` in its folder until its tag,
with no prerelease number and no prerelease tag; `linear-course` is `1.0.0-alpha` in `1/` until
`linear-course/v1.0.0`. "draft" leaves `SPEC.md` and `PROTOCOL.md`: it is the reserved term for
a PR. `specs/linear-course/1/CHANGELOG.md` takes the form of a course's changelog (one line per
entry, ranked by bump, the convention of Changesets, named in the description), with an
« Unreleased » section that becomes `1.0.0` at the tag; during the alpha, entries carry no bump
class.

## Options ruled out

- One rule file for the course and the protocol: they have neither the same readers (the
  checks and the course author; the classroom build) nor the same language (D10).
- One rule per row of a table of fields: hundreds of entries such as "`id` is mandatory"; JSON
  Schema is the standard for it and gives validation and completion in the editor (D7).
- `0.y.z` while the spec moves: two renamings (`1/` to `0/` and back, `@1` to `@0` and back),
  and it works once; the prerelease serves again, a `2.0.0-alpha` prepared in `2/` while `1/`
  serves the courses (D13).
- A prerelease number (`1.0.0-alpha.N`, `1.0.0-draft.N`): no reader tells two prereleases
  apart, a course declares only the MAJOR (D13).
- `rc`: it announces a content almost frozen (D13).
- Keep a Changelog: feyn ranks entries by bump, which the reviewer checks against the claimed
  class, and changing it would touch the changelog of `parcours-po` (D13).

## Sources checked

- The README of `redhat-developer/yaml-language-server`: "Schema definitions can be written
  in JSON (`.json`) or YAML (`.yaml` or `.yml`) format"; it supports draft-04, draft-07,
  2019-09 and 2020-12 (2026-10-30).
- SemVer 2.0.0, rules 3 and 9: a published version's content never changes; a prerelease
  follows the patch after a hyphen and precedes the release (read by the second standards run,
  2026-10-30).

## Consequences

- Nothing reads the spec yet, so breaking its number costs nothing; the choice was made on the
  debt (D13).
- The risk of drift between a description and its rules is held by the writing rule above,
  which `check-spec` can verify (D6). `COURSE.md` keeps its verbal forms of obligation until
  the extraction of #318 sorts each sentence into a rule or description (maintainer,
  2026-10-31).
- The protocol's rules leave `PROTOCOL.md` for `rules/protocol.yaml` at that extraction; the
  teacher's `CLAUDE.md` is then generated by the classroom build (D8).
