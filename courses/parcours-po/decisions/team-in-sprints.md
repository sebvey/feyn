Without this, an agent facing a learner whose team has no sprints would either rewrite 11b,
15b, 15c and 16c with a Kanban variant inside the concept files, which every learner then
reads, or would also send them to annex A2 for every passage where the sprint is only a time
landmark (« une story tient dans un sprint »), so the concepts would grow heavier for every
learner, or A2 would grow without bound.

`parcours-po` assumes a Scrum team working in sprints (`plan.md`, « Parti pris : une équipe en
sprints »). Concept 0 says so in its first message and asks whether the learner's team works
without sprints. Only 11b, 15b, 15c and 16c depend on the sprint: for a learner without sprints,
annex A2 (A2a to A2d) replaces those sub-concepts' cases, and a right answer to the annex
question validates the sub-concept. Elsewhere the sprint is only a landmark: the teacher says
so and takes the team's own cycle (its delivery or review cadence) instead, without opening the
annex. This carries over the old decisions 018, 031 and 033 of `parcours-po`.

## Options ruled out

- A neutral course, Scrum and Kanban on equal terms: the audience is a Scrum team or close to
  one, the storyline team works in sprints, and the teacher would hold two versions of the
  sub-concepts concerned.
- A storyline team working in continuous flow: it loses Sprint Planning, the sprint goal and
  the sprint review, which a PO must know.
- A « sans sprint » paragraph in each concept concerned: scattered, and read by every learner.

## Consequences

- A learner without sprints who stumbles on one of the landmarks signals that A2 needs content
  for it.
