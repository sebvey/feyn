# courses

The courses, one folder each, `courses/<id>/`. A course holds its `course.yaml` (identity, spec version), its plan, glossary, storyline, domains, concepts and decisions. `po-essai`, two concepts, is the course of the tree: the bench every change of the spec is tried on. The founding course, migrated from the frozen repository, is archived at a tag of the history and re-created through the creation path (epic #520, whose Material says where it is read). Course material is in French and is never translated.
