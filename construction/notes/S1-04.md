## Questions for the maintainer, and the choice made meanwhile

1. **"Pourquoi" lines.** The issue says "only where the distillation marked a regression risk";
   the distillation marks none. Choice: a "Pourquoi" line on the 17 rules whose ADR reports the
   old teacher actually doing the unwanted thing in a test or session (ADR 005, 008, 009, 027,
   028, 034, 035, 052, 054, 057, 058, 060, 061, 065, 069, 077; 056 judged borderline, left out).
   Alternative: none at all (the literal reading), or a "risk" column added to the distillation.
2. **Course specifics with no home in the spec.** The old teacher's posture (« Product Owner
   senior qui forme l'apprenant ») and onboarding questions 2 and 3 (« s'il est ou va être PO
   d'un produit », « ce qu'il connaît déjà de Scrum et de la gestion de produit (backlog, user
   story) ») are course-specific, and neither `course.yaml` nor `plan.md` has a field for them.
   Choice: rules 1.1 and 1.6 are generic (a seasoned practitioner of the subject; the role the
   audience aims at; what the learner knows of the subject). Option: an optional `plan.md`
   section (« Le prof »: posture, onboarding questions) the teacher reads, which is a spec MINOR
   plus a course issue. S2-05's onboarding scenario will show whether the generic wording drifts.
   **Ruled 2026-10-27** (decision `specs/linear-course/decisions/onboarding-in-plan.md`, issue
   #87): the questions and the posture belong to the course. `plan.md` has a mandatory section
   « Accueil » (`headings.onboarding`, `SPEC.md` section 6, checked by `course-files`): the
   posture, then the questions, each `**<label>** : <question>`. Rules 1.1, 1.5, 1.6 and 8.5 read
   it, the nickname question stays first in the protocol; each answer is a line
   `{memory.profile_line}` (`- {label} : {answer}`) of `learner/apprenant.md`, replacing the keys
   `memory_headings.role` and `memory_headings.before`. `courses/parcours-po/plan.md` holds the
   old posture and questions 2 and 3, word for word.
3. **`site_url` is a classroom parameter.** The issue's example is `{course.site_url}`; the design
   (`05-teacher-classroom.md`) puts `site_url` in `course.lock`, derived from the channel. Choice:
   `{classroom.site_url}`, plus three page addresses (`classroom.domains_url`, `domain_url` with
   `{n}`, `concept_url` with `{nn}`) that the build derives once the site's routes exist
   (stage 3; the old site used `domaines/`, `domaines/N/`, `concepts/NN/`).
4. **Key syntax.** The issue's example is `{question_lead}`; the pack is grouped (`SPEC.md`
   section 9). Choice: `{group.key}`, one dot (`{phrases.question_lead}`); slots inside values
   have no dot (`{n}`). Leads carry their colon in the value (« Exemple : », « Question pour
   toi : »), because the space before the colon is French typography; the design wrote
   `question_lead: "Question pour toi"` without it.
5. **« -> ».** The old « Style » allowed « -> » as a substitute for an arrow. The protocol bans
   every arrow (`typography.banned`), as S1-06 did for the observation line. Recorded in the
   inheritance table (4.12); S1-05 decides whether `->` enters `typography.banned`.
6. **Behaviours added, marked "new" in the inheritance table.** The third stance, the course's
   own choice (6.1, `SPEC.md` 4.1); the declared diagram and its fallback (4.15, 4.16, design
   `04-diagrams.md`); the glossary's term (4.13, as `plan.md` says). Everything else keeps the
   old behaviour.
7. **Two ambiguities of the old text.** (a) The old order « mini-récap, présentation de domaine,
   puis au plus un sous-concept » conflicted with « aucun concept ne s'enseigne dans le même
   message » as a domain presentation: rule 3.17 follows ADR-068 (a domain presentation, or
   without one at most a sub-concept). (b) At resume, `learner/parcours.md` is read before the
   first message (1.11), and a short sentence precedes any tool call (4.9): left as the old text
   had it; S2-05's resume scenario settles it.
