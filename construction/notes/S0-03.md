# S0-03: two frontmatter keys collide with banned words

Found while writing `docs/decisions/` (task S0-03, night 1, 2026-10-27). Nothing is blocked:
the files follow the design as written.

## What was found

`design/09-decisions.md` fixes the decision frontmatter as `date`, `issue`, `status`, `scope`.
Two of these keys are banned words of the glossary:

- `scope` is listed as "not" for **area**, and its value is exactly an area: the design defines
  it as "the component" the decision binds, and "component" is also banned for area. The
  decisions written here hold an area value (`scope: repo` for `docs/decisions/`), and
  `decision-format.md` says "`scope` (the area it binds)".
- `status` is listed as "not" for **health** and for **stances**. Its meaning here (`accepted`,
  `superseded-by: <slug>`) is neither, so this one is a homonym, not a misuse.

A strict grep of `docs/` for banned words therefore always reports the frontmatter of every
decision, and `/health` will flag them forever unless it skips frontmatter keys.

## Options

1. Keep both keys; `/health` and reviews skip YAML keys. No change.
2. Rename `scope` to `area` in the design and in the seven decisions (one `sed`), keep `status`.
3. Rename both (`area`, and `state` is taken by labels, so something like `standing`).

## Recommendation

Option 2: the key holds an area, so the reserved term is the right name, and the area values
(`repo`, `site`, `teacher`, `spec:<name>`, `course:<id>`) then match the labels one to one.
`status` stays. If you agree: edit `design/09-decisions.md` in a dedicated commit, then one PR
renames the key in `docs/decisions/*.md` and in `/decide` (S0-05) if it already exists.

## Also noted

- The glossary tables were copied as they are in `design/13-glossary.md`; their "Means" column
  uses a few banned words in another sense ("the published website", "the illustration that
  precedes the question", "course material"). Left verbatim, since a task does not edit the
  vocabulary.
- `issue: null` is used for decisions taken without an issue (none existed yet).
