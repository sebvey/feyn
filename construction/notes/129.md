# Note, #129: the night runs are retired

Issues run in the work session, through `/orchestrate`: one contributor agent per issue in its
worktree, an independent review, a corrections pass, and the merge by the maintainer
(`.claude/skills/orchestrate/SKILL.md`). `construction/overnight.md`,
`construction/scripts/overnight-1.js` and `construction/scripts/overnight-2.js` are deleted. Of
the five marked facts of its "Agents in worktrees" section, none was in `/orchestrate` yet
(#128 added only the unmarked lines of the contributor brief); they were moved there, verbatim,
under the same title.

The plan files are frozen and a task never edits the design, so nothing in them was changed.
Where they mention night runs, the mention is superseded: nothing runs at night any more, and
the work they describe runs in a work session.
