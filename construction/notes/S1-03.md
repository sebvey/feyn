## Left to the maintainer (not adopted: they change the design)

- **Rec. 4, stances twice in `concept.md`.** The spec keeps both, as the design does: the body
  section « Consensus et choix d'école » and the frontmatter `stances` + `neighbors`, with a
  review rule that they do not contradict. The prototype already shows drift (`concept 3b`
  against `concept 3`). Dropping the body section is a MAJOR-level structure change: decide
  before S1-09 writes the migration workflow, or file an issue.
- **Rec. 12, `sources.md` cites the bank.** The spec keeps `sources.md` reader-facing and built
  as is (design), so the site will show case titles and hints of expected answers (option a).
  Options b (move bank-only claims to `teaching.md`) and c (the site filters rows) change the
  design. Same timing as above.
