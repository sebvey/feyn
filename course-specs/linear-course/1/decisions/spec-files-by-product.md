Without this, an agent would add a rule as one more sentence of `SPEC.md` prose, beside the
text that explains it, where no check can select it by category or count it (working notes D6).

The files of `linear-course` are split by class of products (decision
`rules-with-level-and-method`), plus the MAJOR's `README.md`; each product has its folder,
`course/` and `teacher/`, which holds its description (its `README.md`), its rules (its
`rules.yaml`) and its own files; the MAJOR's `spec.yaml` declares its name, its MAJOR, the MAJOR
of the spec directives it follows, its version and its language. The MAJOR's `README.md` holds the scope, the files, the
categories, the normative references of its rules and the list of checks; the spec directives
(`spec-directives/1/README.md`) hold what a rule file holds, the conformance clause, levels,
methods, how severity follows, the versions and their normative references; neither holds a rule. `course/README.md`
describes what a course is (the lexicon of the course files and the diagrams included); its rules are in
`course/rules.yaml`, in the language of the spec (decision `language-policy`), and its tables of
fields (`course.yaml`, the `concept.md` frontmatter) are schemas in `course/schemas/*.schema.yaml`, JSON Schema
2020-12 written in YAML, each schema counting as one rule ("`course.yaml` is valid against its
schema"); the rule file keeps what a schema cannot say (the order of the glossary, the contract of a
case, the sources). `teacher/README.md` describes the protocol of the teacher (Purpose, notation,
keys and parameters); its rules are in `teacher/rules.yaml`, in French, the bounded exception of
decision `language-policy`; `teacher/` also holds what builds and tests the teacher, each file
described by the MAJOR's `README.md` (section 2). The rule files are these two; the spec's own
files are verified by the tests of `check-spec`, which are not rules (decision
`rules-with-level-and-method`). A description (the spec directives, the MAJOR's `README.md`,
`course/README.md`, `teacher/README.md`) is informative: it explains and never uses the verbal
forms of obligation; a section of `course/README.md` or `teacher/README.md` whose rules are in a
rule file points to their group, restates none and cites no rule id; the rule files are
normative. The version of the MAJOR, `1.0.0-alpha` until its tag, and the form of its
`CHANGELOG.md` are decision `alpha-version-until-tag` of the spec directives, split from this
one (formerly `spec-files-and-alpha-version`).

## Options ruled out

- One rule file for the course and the protocol: they have neither the same readers (the
  checks and the course author; the classroom build) nor the same language (D10).
- One rule per row of a table of fields: hundreds of entries such as "`id` is mandatory"; JSON
  Schema is the standard for it and gives validation and completion in the editor (D7).

## Sources checked

- The README of `redhat-developer/yaml-language-server`: "Schema definitions can be written
  in JSON (`.json`) or YAML (`.yaml` or `.yml`) format"; it supports draft-04, draft-07,
  2019-09 and 2020-12 (2026-10-30).

## Consequences

- The risk of drift between a description and its rules is held by the writing rule above, which
  `check-spec` can verify (D6). `course/README.md` kept its verbal forms of obligation until the
  extraction of #318 (epic #450) sorted each sentence into a rule or description (maintainer,
  2026-10-31).
- The protocol's rules leave `teacher/README.md` for `teacher/rules.yaml` at that extraction; the
  teacher's `CLAUDE.md` is then generated by the classroom build (D8).
- Amended 2026-11-02 (#463): `SPEC.md` is split and removed, as Material point 3 of epic #454
  settles: what holds for every spec (what a rule file holds, conformance, levels, methods,
  severity, versions) moves to the spec directives, what is `linear-course`'s own (scope, files,
  categories, references of its rules, checks) to the MAJOR's `README.md`.
- Amended 2026-11-02 (#465): `spec-files-and-alpha-version` is split, as Material point 15 of
  epic #454 settles: its "files of a spec" part stays here, renamed `spec-files-by-product`; its
  "alpha version" part, which binds the versions of a spec and of the directives alike, becomes
  decision `alpha-version-until-tag` in `spec-directives/1/decisions/`, with its counterfactual
  (D13), its options ruled out and its consequences. Without this, a contributor preparing the
  directives' version would look for the alpha in a decision of `linear-course` it does not bind.
- Amended 2026-11-03 (#501): `teacher/` also holds `build/`, the half of a classroom build that
  depends on the MAJOR (`render-parts`, which renders the parts of the composition as text, and
  its schema), as the maintainer settled in epic #222 (Material point 6). Without this, the author
  of a second MAJOR would look for its teacher's build at the root, which then breaks or fills
  with versions.
- Amended 2026-11-04 (#531): a section of a product's `README.md` whose rules are in its rule
  file points to their group, restates none and cites no rule id, where a description cited rule
  ids before, as the maintainer settled in epic #450 (Material point 10). Without this, a
  contributor extracting a section would restate each rule in the README prose beside the rule
  file, and the two drift.
- Amended 2026-11-04 (#545): the names "teacher" and "protocol" leave this decision for the
  glossary.
