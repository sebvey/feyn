Without this, an agent at an option refinement, a brainstorm or a triage would read that a change counts only once the teacher teaches it, and would rework or refuse a work item of the site, or of the course without its teacher, that the maintainer put first; and a contributor would rebuild the teacher and run its fresh tests for a change of the spec, spending on the teacher the time the plan gives the site.

Until the brainstorm on the protocol rules (idea #597), the teacher is frozen: it is not kept up to date with the spec and the course, no work item works its behaviour, and no change is judged by whether the teacher teaches it. The yardstick of a change of `linear-fr` is then the bench course alone: `product-lines/linear-fr/1/course/samples/cafe/` follows the change in the same PR and its checks pass on it. The site is the product being built (epic #598). The teacher stays one of the products of feyn and comes back after that brainstorm, which lifts this decision by superseding it with the yardstick it sets. This applies to `docs/vision.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and the reading of an item against the purpose. Decided by the maintainer on 2026-11-05.

## Options ruled out

- Leaving the texts as they are until the brainstorm: every option refinement and every brainstorm would read a purpose the plan contradicts.
- Removing the teacher from `vision.md` altogether: it is still one of the products of feyn, and the brainstorm brings it back.
- Freezing the rule that a decision on how the teacher teaches enters only after two fresh tests, to lift it with this decision: the maintainer dropped it (decision `decision-format`), working the teacher's behaviour being later work whose proof the brainstorm settles.
