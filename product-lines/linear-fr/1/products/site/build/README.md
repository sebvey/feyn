# linear-fr: the published parts of a course, for its site

| Reader | Comes for | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| builder | the published parts of a course as data, for the site engine to lay out | [`render-site`](render-site), [`site.schema.yaml`](site.schema.yaml), "The document" below |
| spec writer | what changes here when the form, the lexicons or the URL scheme change | [`render-site`](render-site), [`test-render`](test-render), [`../../../course/form.yaml`](../../../course/form.yaml) |

The readers are defined in [`spec-directives/README.md`](../../../../../../spec-directives/README.md#the-readers).

The half of a site build that depends on this spec MAJOR: the form of a course (`course/form.yaml`, which says what is published), the keys of the course lexicon, the site's lexicon and its URL scheme are this MAJOR's, so what reads them is versioned with it, as `course/checks/` is. The other half, the site engine, depends on the target only and lives at the root of the repository (`site-builder/`): it runs this folder's `render-site`, validates its output against `site.schema.yaml` and lays the pages out from it, never reading a course file, nor a spec file but this schema. A second MAJOR or a second spec writes its own `products/site/build/`; the root does not change. Nothing in this folder names a mechanism of the engine.

- `render-site`: the rendering, a Node script.
- `site.schema.yaml`: the shape of its output, JSON Schema 2020-12 written in YAML, as `course/schemas/`.
- `test-render`: its test.

## The command

```
mise exec -- product-lines/linear-fr/1/products/site/build/render-site <course-dir> [--form <file>]
```

It reads the course through the parser of this MAJOR, `course/parser.js`, and its readers of the concept references, of the claim marks and of the glossary, `course/refs.js`, `course/claims.js` and `course/glossary.js`, the ones the checks use, given the lib of the spec directives this MAJOR declares, `spec-directives/1/lib/` (with its `md.js` and its `mdast.js`): `course.yaml`, `logo.svg`, `sources.yaml`, `plan.md`, the domain files, each concept folder (`concept.md`, its frontmatter and its diagrams), `storyline.md`, `glossary.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`. It keeps the parts `course/form.yaml` names `published: true`, closed by default: a part the form does not name, or names without `published: true`, is never output, so « Consigne au professeur », « Accueil », the free sections of `plan.md` and « Non publié » never are, nor the quotes of `sources.yaml` and its `checked`, an audit material. It reads the claim marks through `course/claims.js` only, never by a pattern of its own (decision `claim-marks-read-by-one-reader`), and gives those of each concept that stand in a published part, so that the engine renders them with no grammar of a claim mark. It adds the language of the MAJOR's `spec.yaml`, the words of `products/site/lexicon.yaml`, those of `course/lexicon.yaml` the published parts carry (their headings, « Consensus et choix d'école » among them, the kind words, a case's among them, the labels of the fields of a unit, the nature words of a claim mark) and `products/site/url-scheme.yaml`, and prints one JSON document to stdout. It writes nothing and rewrites no course text. It loads the `yaml` package through that lib (`mise exec -- npm ci --prefix spec-directives/1` once).

- `--form <file>`: a form other than `course/form.yaml`, YAML or JSON, so that `test-render` checks the closed default; a site build never gives it.

Exit 0 rendered, 1 an input it cannot read (a course of another spec, a course map with no domain or no concept, a form that does not publish the course map or the logo, a `course.yaml` with no `site.accent_color`, a missing `logo.svg`, `plan.md`, domain file, `concept.md` or diagram SVG, a `sources.yaml` that is no YAML, a course lexicon with no `natures`, a URL scheme without `pages.domain`, `pages.concept`, the form of `{nn}` or `anchors.diagram`, a `spec.yaml` with no `language`), 2 a usage error. A missing `storyline.md`, `glossary.md`, `CHANGELOG.md` or `sources.yaml` is no error: its key is null (`storyline`, `glossary`, `release`) or empty (`sources`). A claim mark the check `sources` fails stays in the text as written, and so does a link the reader does not take as a claim mark.

## The document

Every text is the course's Markdown as written: its claim marks (`[le texte affirmé](consensus:S1,S2 "nom")`) and its concept references (« concept 0b ») stay in it as written, for the engine to render, a claim mark by its destination (a concept's `claim_marks`), a reference by its text (`concept_refs`).

| Key | Holds |
|---|---|
| `spec` | `linear-fr@1` |
| `language` | `language` of the MAJOR's `spec.yaml` (ISO 639-1), the language of its courses and of every page of the site |
| `course` | `id`, `title`, `subtitle`, `annexes` of `course.yaml`; `logo`, the text of the course's `logo.svg` as written (`logo` of `course/form.yaml`); `accent_color`, `site.accent_color` of `course.yaml`, as declared: the engine derives the favicon, the share image and the accent of each theme from them |
| `plan` | the published sections of `plan.md`, each `key` (its lexicon key), `heading`, `text` |
| `domains` | each domain of `course.yaml`, in order: `number`, `slug`, `title`, `objective`, `duration_min`, `concepts` (their numbers), `path` (its address), `sections` (the published sections of its domain file, as in `plan`) |
| `concepts` | each concept and annex of `course.yaml`, in order: `number`, `annex`, `slug`, `title`, `objective`, `prerequisites`, `duration_min`, `domain` (the number of the domain that lists it, null for an annex), `path`; then its published parts: `head` (« L'essentiel »), `sections` (each `id`, `title` and its `units`, each `id`, `title` and its published `blocks`, in the order the site renders them: `lesson` its matter, `cases` each with its `title` (from its heading), its `anchor` and `question`, `revealed` (what the site shows hidden until the reader reveals it, the fields `revealed` of the block of cases in `course/form.yaml`: its `expected_answer`, then its `misconceptions_in_play`, each its `name` and the `id` of the misconception of its unit it names) and `supplementary`, then `misconceptions`, those of the lesson, each its `id` (its anchor on the concept page, the unit's id then its name in lowercase ASCII words joined by hyphens), `name`, `belief`, `root` and `correction`, after the cases so that the site gives none away before the question), `foot` (its `heading` and its published blocks), `diagrams` (each entry with its `anchor`, its id on the concept page, `anchors.diagram` of the URL scheme filled with its file name without `.svg`, and its `svg`, the file as written); and `claim_marks`, the claim marks of its published text in the order of `concept.md` (`claims` of `course/form.yaml`, the parts that state claims), each its `url` (the link's destination as written, the one the engine sees on the same link), `nature` (its key in the lexicon's `natures`: `consensus`, `school`, `fact`, `course`), `sources` (their ids), `name` (the link's title, null with none), `holder` (`claims.<concept>/<name>.holder` of `sources.yaml`, when it gives one), `text` (the plain text claimed) and `anchor` (the id of its place on the concept page: its misconception, else its unit, else the key of its part, `essentials`, `landmarks`, `deepening`) |
| `storyline` | `world`, its only key: its H1 `title`, its `text`, its `sections` of free titles; the rest of `storyline.md` is the teacher's, never published |
| `glossary` | `table`, its first table (`header`, `rows`), and its published `sections` (« Termes à éviter »), each its table |
| `sources` | the sources of `sources.yaml`, in the order of their ids, each its `id`, its CSL fields as written (`title`, `author`, `container-title`, `issued`, `URL`, `DOI`, `accessed`…), its CSL `type` (its own, else the one its kind gives, `sources_file.csl_type` of `course/form.yaml`), its `kind`, `control` and `independence`, words of the course lexicon; never its `quotes` nor its `checked`. No key when the form does not publish `sources.yaml` |
| `release` | the latest release of `CHANGELOG.md`, `version` and `date`; null while it has none, the site then writing its own « version non publiée » |
| `concept_refs` | each distinct concept reference of the published text, its `text` as written and its `targets`: each `id` with the `concept` it names, that concept's `path` and the `anchor` (the id of a section or a unit, null for a whole concept) |
| `words` | `course`, the words of `course/lexicon.yaml` the published parts carry, by group and key (`headings`, `block_kinds`, `unit_blocks`, `unit_fields`, `natures`); `site`, `products/site/lexicon.yaml` as written |
| `url_scheme` | `products/site/url-scheme.yaml` as written; `path` and a diagram's `anchor` above are already filled from it |

## The test

`mise exec -- product-lines/linear-fr/1/products/site/build/test-render` runs `render-site` on `cafe`, the spec's sample course (`product-lines/linear-fr/1/course/samples/cafe/`), validates the document against `site.schema.yaml` (ajv's `Ajv2020`), and checks what the schema cannot say: the language is that of `spec.yaml`, the logo is `logo.svg` as written and the accent colour that of `course.yaml`, a fragment of each published part reaches it, a claim mark of concept 0 with its destination, its nature, its sources and its unit, the count of claim marks per concept, those of `check-course`, « Consensus et choix d'école » under `headings.stances` with its three columns, a claim mark of each stance in concept 0, its school claim with its holder, no fact with a holder, the source `S1` with its CSL fields, its type from its kind, its kind, control and independence, and no quote, every source in the order of its id and every source a claim mark cites among them, the SVG as written, each diagram's anchor, `schema-` then its file name without `.svg`, a concept reference resolved, the misconceptions of every unit after its cases, each with its id, its name, its belief, its « Racine » and its « Correction »; each case of `cafe` under the title of its heading, in the order of the concept file, its expected answer and « Idées fausses en jeu » inside `revealed` and nowhere else, each name in play linking to the id of the misconception of its unit of that name; the storyline holds its world only; no unpublished part does (each « Consigne au professeur », « Accueil », a free section of `plan.md`, a quote of `sources.yaml`, « Faits fixés » of `storyline.md`, the introduction of `glossary.md`, « Non publié »); the closed default, one case each, on a copy of the form: a part named `published: false`, a part named without `published`, a part the form does not name (« Approfondissement », its claim marks with it), a file the form does not name, `sources.yaml` named `published: false`, and a form that does not name `logo.svg`, which `render-site` refuses (the site has no default logo); on a copy of `cafe` given two releases, the latest one, and a source and a quote that carry `checked`, neither date in the document. Exit 0 when every check passes. `check-spec` does not run it.
