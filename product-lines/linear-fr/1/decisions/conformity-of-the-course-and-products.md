Without this, an agent would call a course conforming because it passes the blocking checks, and give a check of the spec's own files a rule and a category of its own.

A rule binds the course, its diagrams included, or a product, the teacher today; each conforms when it holds every requirement of its own. A course conforms when it holds every requirement of `course/rules.yaml`, whatever its method: the blocking checks are a necessary condition, not a sufficient one, the review and taught-check do the rest, and a course tag certifies the whole. A teacher conforms when it holds every protocol rule of `products/teacher/rules.yaml`, each judged as its `criterion` or its `check` says (`products/teacher/README.md`); for now the maintainer judges a session played on the bench course (decision `teacher-yardstick-cafe-and-maintainer-session`). Recommendations are outside conformity (the ISO sense of "should"). The lexicons are neither the course nor a product: they live in the spec's folder, written by its authors. The spec's own files (the rule files, the schemas, the slugs of the rules, the lexicons, `spec.yaml` and its version) are verified by the tests of the spec directives, run by `check-spec`, which are not rules: they carry no category and no level, each failure blocks `check-spec`, and none counts toward the conformity of the course or of a product.

## Consequences

- No teacher conforms today: no session has been judged against every protocol rule; the fresh tests, which judged only the rules a session of the spec's sample course exercised on their path (idea #509), sleep in the teacher implementation, their scenarios out of the spec (decision `teacher-yardstick-cafe-and-maintainer-session`).
- Amended 2026-10-31 (#339): a rule binds a product, a course or a teacher; the consistency of the spec's own files is verified by the tests of `check-spec`, which are not rules; the categories are those of a course and the families of the protocol. Without this, a contributor applies "every check verifies a rule" to a check of the spec's files and writes a rule file and a category for them, as #329 did (a third rule file, a sixth category named after the spec, and a marker so that `check-course` ran it on a course).
- Amended 2026-11-03 (#497): the composition of the teacher joins the spec's own files that the tests of `check-spec` verify, by a test that checks the form of the file. Without this, a contributor reads the composition as a rule of the teacher and gives it a category and a level, or leaves its test outside the clause.
- Amended 2026-11-04 (#545): "conformity", "conformance clause", and the French words of a diagram and of a schema, leave for the glossary (decision `word-choices-in-the-glossary`).
- Amended 2026-11-08 (#730): renamed after the course and the products, and reworded, the clause unchanged: the course is the input of the builds of its products, not a product, so a rule binds the course or a product, the teacher today (decision `product-line-vocabulary`; epic #729, Material point 3). Without this, a contributor would count the course among the products of feyn, beside the site and the teacher, and write a folder of products that holds the course.
- Amended 2026-11-09 (#765): the decision moves from the spec directives to this spec (`decisions/` of `linear-fr`, MAJOR 1), which holds it now: the directives keep only what their tools read (decision `directives-hold-what-the-tools-read`), and the clause of the course is written in this spec's README beside its levels; the composition and the test scenarios of the teacher are no longer among the spec's own files the tests of `check-spec` verify, their tests gone from the directives. Without this, a contributor would look for when a course conforms in the directives, which no longer know the levels a requirement belongs to.
- Amended 2026-11-09 (#767): a teacher conforms when it holds every protocol rule of `products/teacher/rules.yaml`, each judged as its `criterion` or its `check` says, for now by the maintainer on a session played on the bench course (decision `teacher-yardstick-cafe-and-maintainer-session`), no longer by every requirement verified by demonstration through the fresh tests.
