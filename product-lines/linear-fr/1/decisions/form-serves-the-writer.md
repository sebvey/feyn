Without this, a spec writer would add to the form of a course a structure that is hard to fill (a source required for every sentence, a status written on every item, a long list of mandatory fields to describe a source), so the creator of a course, most often an agent, fills it badly or not at all, and the writing of a course waits on research instead of moving.

The form of a course of `linear-fr` (its files, their fields, what its checks require) serves the creation and the maintenance of a course, and never hinders them. A structure is required only when what it holds cannot be left out without the course lying; what makes a course better without being required is recommended and reported, not failed: a claim is marked when its writer can, a source is described by the few fields that identify it, and what no human has verified yet is counted, not refused (decisions `claim-marked-in-the-text`, `sources-a-course-file`, `source-described-by-kind-control-independence`). A principle posed by the maintainer, a decision of the spec, the most local area it binds, not a rule of a course: it binds the writing of the spec's forms, which a rule of a course does not.

## Options ruled out

- A rule of the course rules alone: it binds a course, not the spec writer who adds the next field.
- A decision of the spec directives: they hold only what their tools read.
- Every structure required, for completeness: a field hard to fill is filled badly, which is worse than empty.
